Key Innovations

Engineered for Market Accuracy

Traditional used phone listings rely on rough guesswork. PhoneValue AI brings rigor through statistical feature engineering and non-linear regression modeling.

Random Forest Ensemble

Combines hundreds of independent decision trees to capture non-linear relationships like steep initial year-one depreciation versus gradual late-stage leveling.

Hardware Degradation Metric

Explicitly models battery cycle aging and cosmetic conditions (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) to penalize or reward well-maintained hardware.

Defensive Market Bounds

Ensures realistic valuation guards—preventing outputs from exceeding realistic ceilings (98% of MSRP) or dipping below scrap recovery value.

Step-by-Step Pipeline

How the Inference Engine Works

From raw phone specs to an explainable valuation in four reproducible steps.

Step 01

Feature Ingestion

User submits brand, age in months, RAM, internal storage, battery health percentage, physical condition, and original MSRP.

Step 02

Preprocessing & Scaling

Categorical labels are converted via serialized label encoders, and continuous features are transformed through pre-trained StandardScaler.

Step 03

Ensemble Inference

The scikit-learn model evaluates feature interactions and outputs the expected market equilibrium resale price.

Step 04

Valuation Report

Computes fair price confidence bounds, depreciation percentage, retained equity, and monthly rate of value decay.
